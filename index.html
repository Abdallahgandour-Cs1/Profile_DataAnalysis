About Me

I am a passionate Data Analyst and a Third-Year Computer Science student at the Faculty of Computers and Information. Currently, I am a trainee at the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI) in the Data Analysis Track, where I am strengthening my analytical and technical skills through hands-on projects and real-world case studies.

I specialize in data cleaning, transformation, visualization, and statistical analysis. My goal is to help organizations make data-driven decisions by uncovering meaningful insights and hidden patterns within data. I am proficient in Python, SQL, Power BI, and Excel, and continuously working on enhancing my problem-solving and analytical thinking skills.
